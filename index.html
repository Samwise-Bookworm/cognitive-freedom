THE MOVEMENT FOR COGNITIVE FREEDOM

Defending the freedom to think.

What is Cognitive Freedom?

Cognitive freedom is the ability of a person to direct their attention, form their beliefs and make decisions without undue manipulation or coercion.

This project examines the forces that threaten that freedom and develops principles for protecting it.

Manifest

We believe that freedom requires more than the absence of physical coercion. It requires the ability to think, judge and choose for oneself.
